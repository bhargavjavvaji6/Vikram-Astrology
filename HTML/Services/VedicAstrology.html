What Is Vedic Astrology?

Vedic Astrology, also known as Jyotish Shastra, is one of the oldest systems of astrology, originating in ancient India. It uses your date of birth, time of birth, and place of birth to create a detailed birth chart and analyze planetary influences throughout your life. Unlike general astrology, Vedic Astrology focuses on the precise positions of the planets, zodiac signs, houses, and planetary periods (Dashas) to provide personalized guidance about different aspects of life.
