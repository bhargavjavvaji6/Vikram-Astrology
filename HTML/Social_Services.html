Vikram Astrologer Community Trust & Social Services

At Vikram Astrologer Community Trust, we believe in giving back to society through meaningful social service initiatives that support individuals, families, and communities. Our mission extends beyond astrology and spiritual guidance by helping people through charitable programs focused on food assistance, education, wellness, healthcare awareness, and community support. We are committed to making a positive difference by providing resources and assistance to those who need it most. Through our community-focused programs, we strive to promote compassion, well-being, education, and social responsibility.

Food Donation Programs

Providing food donation support and community assistance to families facing hardship and daily nutritional challenges.

Education Support

Supporting students through educational assistance, learning resources, skill development, and academic growth opportunities.

Meditation & Wellness

Promoting meditation, mindfulness, wellness education, stress management, and personal development within the community.

Youth Development

Empowering young individuals through leadership programs, education support, mentoring, and career development initiatives.

Senior Citizen Support

Providing senior citizen assistance, social engagement programs, wellness support, and community care services.

Community Welfare Services

Delivering community welfare programs, charitable support, social assistance, and outreach services for those in need.

Volunteer & Charity Programs

Encouraging volunteer participation, charitable activities, community service, and social responsibility initiatives.

Emergency Relief Support

Offering emergency relief assistance, essential resources, and community support during unexpected crisis situations.
